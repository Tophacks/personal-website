Choose a photo or enable the camera. Person detection runs on every third camera frame, adapted from the original project's scheduling strategy.

What runs here?

TensorFlow.js COCO-SSD identifies the person class on your device. It is a different pretrained model from the original Python/OpenCV HOG + SVM program.

Model code and weights are downloaded from a public CDN/model host. Uploaded photos and video frames are not sent to your portfolio's server.

From the desktop prototype to the web

ORIGINAL SOURCEPython + OpenCV + Tkinter

Capture threads, bounded queue of five frames, every-third-frame processing, HOG + SVM pedestrian detector.

BROWSER ADAPTATIONJavaScript + TensorFlow.js

User-initiated photo/webcam processing, every-third-frame detection, on-device COCO-SSD people bounding boxes.

Detection can miss or falsely flag people. Results are a demonstration of computer vision, not surveillance-grade verification or a calibrated safety system.
