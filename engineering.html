02 / Distributed simulation

TransitOps

An experimental transit-operations simulator for exploring how vehicles, stations, signal controllers, and passenger demand could coordinate in disrupted service.

PythonDiscrete-event simulationWeb prototypesTransit operations
passenger demand + disruptions ↓ stations / buses / signals ↓ dispatch and priority decisions ↓ simulation metrics + visualization

System design

Model the network as interacting actors with local information and shared operating constraints. Compare decisions such as dispatching an extra bus or prioritizing a delayed service at an intersection.

Evaluation plan

Record passenger wait time, service delay, vehicle utilization, and fuel or energy estimates against a fixed baseline. Improvements should be reported only after reproducible runs.

Prototype and research work in progress; not a production transit-control system.

03 / Machine learning and control

NeuroSim 2.0

A simulation and reinforcement-learning experiment focused on control policies, including rocket-landing-style scenarios.

PythonReinforcement learningPPO experimentsPolicy evaluation
simulated state → policy → control action ↑ ↓ └──── environment step ┘ ↓ reward / crash / timeout / error

What to measure

Success and crash rates, touchdown error, fuel use, episode duration, and robustness across initial conditions. Evaluate on episodes separate from training.

Why failures matter

A training run can achieve a better reward while producing worse operational results. The notebook should preserve failed policies and explain reward-design changes rather than claim success from training reward alone.

Experimental research software; not a validated physical flight-control system.
