Founder project / Social technology

Kinora

Life is meant to be experienced, not compared.

Inspired by the joy of playing together, Kinora reimagines digital social interaction around shared experiences rather than passive scrolling.

Private prototype · In development
Kinora trailer

Promotional video coming soon.

A different kind of social platform

Kinora is designed around meaningful connections, shared activities, virtual spaces and spontaneous conversation. The aim is to give people more ways to spend time together without relying on swipes, popularity scores or endless feeds.

The experience

Social spaces and shared activities

Meet people, spend time with friends, and interact with clearly identified AI characters in an evolving virtual environment.

The technology

Character cognition

An independently developed cognitive engine combines mathematical affect modelling, probabilistic appraisal, personality, values and behavioural selection with language and voice generation.

From cognition to expression

PerceiveInterpret conversational signals and uncertainty
DecideCharacter state, values and behavioural choices
ExpressLanguage, voice and animation

Conceptual architecture, not a performance benchmark. The experimental Python and neural-computation direction is under development.

What comes next

Improve conversational realism, independent character behaviour and voice integration, followed by private user evaluation. Specialized neural perception and real-time acoustic interpretation are planned, not validated product capabilities.

Development status

Kinora is in private development. No public launch, adoption figures or validated performance claims are presented here.
